FAQ

No fine print.

How long does it take to build a website?
A landing page takes around 10 business days. A full corporate site usually runs 3 to 5 weeks, and an e-commerce store or custom system, 6 to 10 weeks. The exact timeline comes out of the planning phase, right after discovery — and from that point it becomes a commitment, with a schedule you can follow.
How much does a Digify project cost?
It depends on scope, which is why we don't publish a price list. We run a free diagnosis, understand what the project needs to achieve, and send a proposal with fixed scope, timeline and price. If the budget doesn't make sense for where your company is right now, we'll say so before you invest any more time.
How long until SEO shows results?
The first signals — indexing, long-tail ranking gains and rising impressions — usually appear within 60 to 90 days. Consolidated results on competitive terms take 6 to 12 months. SEO is an asset, not an ad: the return compounds and keeps paying after you stop investing.
Do you provide maintenance after launch?
Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support window, and we offer monthly maintenance plans covering updates, backups, performance monitoring and small content changes. You won't be left with a site that quietly rots six months after deploy.
What technologies do you work with?
We pick the stack based on the problem, not on preference. WordPress and WooCommerce when the client needs editorial autonomy, static sites when performance and SEO are the top priority, and custom development in PHP, Python and JavaScript when the project involves integrations and business rules of its own.
Do you work with clients outside Brazil?
Yes. We work remotely with clients across the Americas and Europe. Discovery, check-ins, deliveries and support all run over video calls, chat and shared access to the project environment. Our team is based in Brazil, which overlaps with US business hours — one of the reasons clients use us as a nearshore option.
